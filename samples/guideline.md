# Bengaluru Community Learning Fund — 2027 Small Grants Guideline

## 1. Eligibility
Applicants must be registered non-profit organisations (Trust, Society or Section 8 company) operating in Karnataka.
Applicants must have been operating for at least two years at the date of application.
Organisations with an annual income above ₹2 crore are not eligible.
Individuals and for-profit companies will not be considered.

## 2. Funding
Grants range from ₹2,00,000 to ₹10,00,000 per project.
Projects must be completed within 12 months of the grant start date.
Overheads should not exceed 15% of the total requested amount.
Co-funding from other sources is encouraged but not required.

## 3. What the application must contain
The application must include a project summary of no more than 300 words.
The application must describe the target beneficiaries and the expected number of people reached.
The application must include a month-by-month activity plan.
The application must include an itemised budget.
Applicants should describe how the project will be sustained after the grant ends.
Applicants are encouraged to explain how they will measure learning outcomes.

## 4. Supporting documents
A copy of the registration certificate is required.
Audited financial statements for the last two financial years are required.
A letter of support from at least one partner school or library is required.
A CV of the project lead is recommended.

## 5. Submission
Applications must be submitted in English.
Applications received after 31 January 2027 will not be considered.
