# Application — Reading Corners for Government Schools

**Organisation:** Akshara Reading Trust, a registered public charitable trust in Bengaluru, Karnataka (registered 2019).

## Project summary
Akshara Reading Trust will set up reading corners in 15 government primary schools in East Bengaluru. Each corner gets 300 graded Kannada and English books, a trained volunteer, and two reading sessions a week. The project builds on our pilot in 4 schools, where reading fluency improved by 40% in one term. We are partnering with the Whitefield Public Library, which will rotate books every quarter.

## Beneficiaries
The project will reach about 2,400 children in classes 1 to 5, most from first-generation learner families.

## Activity plan
Months 1–2: school agreements and book procurement. Months 3–4: volunteer training. Months 5–12: reading sessions and quarterly book rotation.

## Budget
We request ₹9,50,000. Books ₹4,50,000; volunteer stipends ₹3,00,000; training ₹80,000; administration ₹1,20,000.

## After the grant
We hope the schools will continue the sessions.

## Organisation
Our annual income in 2025-26 was ₹1.4 crore. We have run literacy programmes since 2019 and have been recognised by the State Education Department.
