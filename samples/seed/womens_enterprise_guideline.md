# Karnataka Women's Enterprise Fund — 2027 Call for Proposals

## 1. Who can apply
Applicants must be women-led self-help groups (SHGs), SHG federations or registered non-profits working with SHGs.
The applicant must be registered and have a bank account in the organisation's name.
At least 75% of project beneficiaries must be women from households below the poverty line.
Applicants must have completed at least one livelihood project in the last three years.
Projects in urban municipal corporation areas are not eligible.

## 2. Grant size and duration
Grants of ₹5,00,000 to ₹15,00,000 are available for projects of 12 to 18 months.
At least 60% of the budget must be spent directly on equipment, raw material or training for beneficiaries.
A beneficiary contribution (cash or in kind) of at least 10% is strongly encouraged.

## 3. Proposal content
The proposal must describe the enterprise activity and the local demand for the product or service.
The proposal must state how many women will be trained and how many will earn an income by the end of the project.
The proposal must include a market linkage plan naming at least one buyer, retailer or marketplace.
The proposal must include a 12-month financial projection showing when the enterprise breaks even.
Applicants should explain how the enterprise will be governed and how profits will be shared among members.
Applicants are encouraged to describe any digital payment or bookkeeping tools the group will use.

## 4. Attachments
A copy of the SHG or organisation registration certificate is required.
Bank statements for the last six months are required.
A letter from the training partner confirming the training plan is required.
Photographs of the proposed work site are recommended.

## 5. Submission
Proposals must be submitted in English or Kannada.
Proposals must be submitted through the district office before 15 March 2027.
