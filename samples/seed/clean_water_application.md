# Application — Solar Water ATMs for Six Villages in Raichur

**Organisation:** Jaladhara Foundation, Raichur — a registered trust working on rural water and sanitation since 2018.

## The problem
Six villages in Lingsugur taluk (populations between 1,800 and 4,200) depend on borewells with high fluoride and TDS. Many families report joint pain and dental fluorosis, and children miss school because of water-borne illness.

## What we will do
We will install six community water ATMs with reverse-osmosis (RO) units, one per village, each producing 1,000 litres an hour. Each ATM will run on a 5 kW rooftop solar system with grid backup. Households will use prepaid cards to collect 20 litres a day.

## User fee
Users will pay ₹5 for 20 litres. This covers electricity backup, filter replacement and the operator's salary.

## Budget
We request ₹23,80,000 over 15 months: RO units and ATMs ₹10,20,000; solar systems ₹6,60,000; civil works ₹2,40,000; community mobilisation ₹1,60,000; project staff ₹2,00,000; monitoring ₹1,00,000.

## Community involvement
Village water committees will be formed in each village. Our earlier plants in Sindhanur taluk have run for four years without a single breakdown.

## Results expected
About 3,900 households will have safe drinking water within a year, and fluoride-related illness will fall by half.
