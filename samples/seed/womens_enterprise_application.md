# Proposal — Sahaya Mahila Sangha Tailoring and Garment Unit, Tumakuru

**Applicant:** Sahaya Mahila Sangha Federation, a registered federation of 18 SHGs in Gubbi taluk, Tumakuru district (registered 2016).

## Enterprise activity
We will set up a shared tailoring and garment unit in Gubbi town producing school uniforms and cotton bags. The unit will have 25 industrial sewing machines, two cutting tables and a finishing area. Demand is strong: government and private schools in the taluk buy about 30,000 uniforms every year, mostly from suppliers in Bengaluru.

## Women reached
We will train 60 women over the first six months. By the end of the project we expect 45 women to be earning a regular income from the unit.

## Market linkage
We are in discussion with schools in the taluk and with a cotton bag wholesaler in Tumakuru city.

## Budget
We request ₹12,40,000 over 15 months. Sewing machines and tables ₹6,20,000; raw material for the first three production cycles ₹2,10,000; trainer fees ₹1,50,000; rent ₹1,20,000; coordinator salary ₹1,40,000.

## Financial plan
The unit will become profitable within the first year.

## Track record
Between 2022 and 2024 the federation ran a dairy collection project with 140 members, which raised average household income by 35%. All our members are from BPL households.

## Governance
A five-member committee elected from the SHGs will manage the unit.
