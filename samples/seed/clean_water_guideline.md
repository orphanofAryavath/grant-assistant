# Rural Clean Water Challenge 2027 — Grant Guidelines

## Eligibility
Applicants must be registered non-profit organisations with at least three years of work in water, sanitation or rural development.
Projects must be located in villages with a population under 5,000.
Applicants must not have an unresolved audit objection from any previous grant.

## Funding
The maximum grant is ₹25,00,000 per project.
Projects must be completed and handed over to the community within 18 months.
Capital costs must not exceed 70% of the total grant.

## What the proposal must cover
The proposal must identify the water source and present baseline water-quality test results for each village.
The proposal must describe the treatment technology and why it suits the local water quality.
The proposal must include an operations and maintenance plan covering at least five years after handover, including who pays for repairs.
The proposal must state the user fee, if any, and how households that cannot pay will be supported.
The proposal must describe how the Gram Panchayat will be involved.
Applicants should include a plan for regular water-quality testing after the plant is running.
Applicants are encouraged to use solar or other renewable energy where feasible.

## Required documents
A no-objection certificate (NOC) from each Gram Panchayat is required.
Audited financial statements for the last three years are required.
Baseline water-quality test reports from an accredited laboratory are required.

## Submission
Applications received after 28 February 2027 will not be considered.
